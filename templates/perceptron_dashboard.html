🧠 What is a Perceptron?

A perceptron is the simplest neural network: it learns a straight line that separates two groups of data. Give it examples, and it finds the dividing boundary.

⚙️ How Does It Learn?

It adjusts its weights by comparing predictions to actual answers. When wrong, it moves the line slightly. Repeat until all examples are correct.

✓ When Does It Work?

When data can be split by a straight line (AND, OR gates work). When it can't (like XOR), you need deeper networks.
